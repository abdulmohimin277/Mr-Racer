HOW TO PLAY

←→
Steer

Move between lanes — also A / D

↑W
Accelerate

Go faster, more distance = more score

XSHIFT
Drift

Hold to slide & cut corners sharper. Earns drift points. Watch for oil slicks!

SPACE
Blaster

Shoot traffic & barriers. Kills refill your gun charge.

C
Cockpit view

Toggle the in-car dashboard & steering wheel. On touch devices use the VIEW button.

PESC
Pause

R = quick restart, M = mute, N = refill blaster

⛁
Coins

Collect golden coins for bonus points.

♥
Lives

You have 3 lives. Collisions cost one.

◆
Combos

Near-misses & blaster kills build a COMBO multiplier up to ×4 — keep it alive for big scores!

TAP
Touch

On phones/tablets use the on-screen buttons. Tap GAS once to cruise, tap again to release.

NEONRUSH

3D HIGHWAY RACER

A fully 3D highway racing game built with JavaScript & Three.js — dodge traffic, drift for boosts, grab coins, and blast obstacles with your twin nose blasters.

WHAT THE GAME CAN DO
  • 3D racing — full 3D highway on a single flat road
  • Garage — 6 racing cars with 8 paint colours
  • Drift & boost — slide corners, release a speed-boost pop
  • Day / night — DAY, NIGHT or a full CYCLE lighting
  • Blasters — shoot traffic & barriers (hits refill ammo)
  • Combos — near-misses & kills build a ×4 multiplier
  • 3 difficulties — Easy, Medium, Hard with dense traffic
  • Camera — chase or cockpit view, always fixed ahead
  • Keyboard & touch — plays with keys or on-screen buttons
  • Best score — saved on your device
